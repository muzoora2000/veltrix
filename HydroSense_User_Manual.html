🌊 1. System Overview

HydroSense is Uganda's integrated digital platform for water resource management and environmental monitoring, developed for the Ministry of Water & Environment. It connects citizens, field technicians, district water officers, scientists, community committees, and national administrators on a single secure platform — enabling real-time reporting, monitoring, coordination, and accountability.

📡
Real-Time Monitoring

Live IoT sensor feeds, water quality readings, and climate data updated every 30 seconds.

📋
Community Reporting

Citizens report water issues via app, SMS, voice, or USSD in 10+ local languages.

🤖
AI-Powered Intelligence

Gemini AI analyses incidents, predicts risks, classifies reports, and supports decision-making.

🗺️
GIS Mapping

Spatial visualization of water points, drought zones, flood risks, and pollution hotspots.

🏛️
Governance & Audit

Full budget tracking, audit logs, and transparency dashboards for public accountability.

🔒
Secure by Design

JWT authentication, bcrypt passwords, role-based access control, and full audit trails.

Supported Devices: HydroSense runs in any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, and smartphone. No app download required. Recommended browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari.

👥 2. User Roles Explained

HydroSense uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Every user has exactly one role. Your role determines which pages you can access and what actions you can perform.

👑 National Admin
Full system access. Manages all users, districts, and settings nationwide.
🏛️ District Officer
District Water Office staff. Manages their district, creates committee accounts, reviews reports.
🔧 Technician
Field Engineer. Handles sensor maintenance, task assignments, and infrastructure repairs.
🏥 Health Officer
Water quality analyst. Tracks disease outbreaks and water safety incidents.
🌍 Climate Scientist
Environmental monitoring. Tracks drought, flood, and climate risk indicators.
🌿 NGO Officer
Environmental advocacy. Community outreach, pollution reporting, and water monitoring.
🏘️ Community Committee
Local water committee member. Logs in with a Committee ID (HSC-CC-…) assigned by their District Officer.
👤 Citizen
General public. Reports water issues, tracks their submissions, receives community alerts.
Note for Community Committee Members: You do NOT self-register. Your account is created by your District Water Officer. You will receive a Committee ID in the format HSC-CC-KAM-2026-0001. Use this ID — not an email address — to log in.

🔐 3. Registration & Login

3.1 Citizen Registration

Only citizens self-register. All other roles are created by system administrators.

  1. Go to veltrix-4r2c.vercel.app and click "Create an Account" or visit /register
  2. Enter your Full Name and Phone Number
  3. Enter your Email Address
  4. Select your District from the dropdown (all 141 Uganda districts available)
  5. Optionally enter your Sub-County and Village / Area
  6. Choose your Preferred Language (Luganda, Swahili, Luo, Runyankore, and more)
  7. Set a Password (minimum 6 characters) and confirm it
  8. Click "Create Citizen Account" — you are automatically logged in

3.2 Email Login (Staff & Citizens)

  1. Visit /login and select the "Email Login" tab (default)
  2. Enter your Email Address and Password
  3. Tick "Remember me" to stay logged in for 365 days (otherwise 7 days)
  4. Click "Sign In"
  5. You are redirected to your role-specific Dashboard

3.3 Committee ID Login

  1. Visit /login and click the "Committee ID" tab
  2. Enter your Committee ID (format: HSC-CC-KAM-2026-0001)
  3. Enter your temporary password given by your District Officer
  4. Click "Sign In"

3.4 Forgot Password

  1. On the Login page, click "Forgot Password?"
  2. Enter your registered email address
  3. Check your inbox for a 6-digit OTP code
  4. Enter the OTP on the verification page
  5. Set and confirm your new password

3.5 Changing Your Password

  1. Click your profile avatar (top-right corner)
  2. Select "Profile Settings"
  3. Scroll to the Change Password section
  4. Enter your current password, then your new password
  5. Click "Update Password"
QR Code Verification: Staff members have a public profile accessible via QR code at /verify/{user-id}. Citizens can scan a staff member's ID badge to verify their identity and role.

📊 4. Dashboards

When you log in, HydroSense automatically shows you a dashboard tailored to your role.

Citizen Dashboard

Shown to users with role: citizen

  • Active Alerts — water and environmental alerts in your district
  • My Reports — quick count of your submitted reports and their statuses
  • Nearby Water Sources — functional boreholes and taps near you
  • Quick-Action Tiles — one-tap access to Report Issue, Multilingual Report, Track My Reports, Water Infrastructure, Water Network, Community Hub
  • Recent Reports — list of your recently submitted reports
  • Announcements — latest public notices from your district

Community Committee Dashboard

Shown to users with role: community_committee

  • Committee Overview — your committee's name, district, and member count
  • Recent Meetings — upcoming and past scheduled meetings
  • Active Incidents — water issues being managed by your committee
  • Projects — ongoing water and sanitation projects in your area
  • Announcements — posted notices for your committee

Main Staff Dashboard

Shown to: District Officer, Technician, Health Officer, Climate Scientist, NGO Officer, National Admin

  • Live Clock & Weather — real-time date, time, and local weather
  • Emergency Alerts — count of critical, warning, and info alerts
  • Water Infrastructure — total points, functional %, offline sensors
  • Maintenance Summary — pending and overdue requests
  • Community Reports — new reports awaiting review
  • Health Incidents — active disease case count
  • Quick Actions — role-specific shortcut buttons
  • Recent Activity Feed — latest system events

👤 5. Citizen Guide

As a Citizen, you can report water and environmental problems in your community, track the status of your submissions, view water sources near you, and participate in community discussions.

5.1 Submitting a Water/Environmental Report

Standard Report (/citizen-report)

  1. Click "Report Issue" from your dashboard or navigate to /citizen-report
  2. Select the incident type: Water Pollution, Illegal Dumping, Sewage Leak, Flooding, Environmental Hazard, Infrastructure Damage, Water Contamination, or Disease Report
  3. Enter a description of what you observed
  4. Set your location (tap "Use My Location" or type it manually)
  5. Optionally check "Report Anonymously"
  6. Attach photos, videos, or audio as evidence
  7. Click "Submit Report"

Multilingual Report (/multilingual-report)

  1. Click "Multilingual Report" or visit /multilingual-report
  2. Step 1 — Details: Select language, incident type, enter description in your local language
  3. Tap "Translate to English" for automatic translation
  4. Record a voice note if you prefer speaking to typing
  5. Step 2 — Media: Attach photos or videos
  6. Step 3 — Review: Confirm details and submit

5.2 Tracking Your Reports

  1. Go to /track-reports or tap "Track My Reports" on your dashboard
  2. View all reports you have submitted with their current status
  3. Click any report to see the full status timeline: Submitted → Under Investigation → Assigned to Technician → Resolved
  4. Use the filter buttons to show only Pending, Investigating, or Resolved reports

5.3 Community Hub (/citizen-hub)

  • Discussions — browse and join conversations on Water Quality, Pollution, Climate, Health, Events, Governance, and General topics
  • Start a Discussion — tap the blue button, choose a category, write your post, and optionally attach media or a voice note
  • Like & Reply — engage with other community members' posts
  • Volunteer Events — view upcoming clean-up or water awareness events and click "Join"
  • Achievements — earn badges for reporting issues, joining events, and community participation

5.4 Guardian Water Network — GWN (/gwn)

GWN is a citizen-science pollution tracking system. You can report environmental hazards and vote on the severity of others' reports.

  • Report types include: Illegal Dumping, Sewage Leak, Oil Spill, Algae Bloom, Dead Fish, Discolouration, Bad Odour, Flood, Broken Infrastructure, Industrial Waste
  • Browse the Feed, view Hotspot pollution areas, and check Statistics by district
  • Tap the 👍 icon on a report to vote and confirm its severity

5.5 Water Infrastructure (/water-infrastructure)

Citizens can view water points (boreholes, taps, springs) in their area — including their status (functional, needs repair, non-functional), type, and number of beneficiaries. Tap a water point for full technical details.

🏘️ 6. Community Committee Member Guide

Your account is created by your District Water Officer. You will receive a Committee ID (e.g. HSC-CC-KAM-2026-0001) and a temporary password. Use these to log in via the "Committee ID" tab on the login page.

6.1 Accessing Committee Management

  1. Log in with your Committee ID and password
  2. Navigate to /committee-management from the sidebar
  3. You will see your committee's dashboard with KPI cards: total members, upcoming meetings, active incidents, and running projects

6.2 Scheduling a Meeting

  1. In Committee Management, select your committee and open the Meetings tab
  2. Click "Schedule Meeting"
  3. Enter: meeting title, date, time, location, agenda, and type (regular, emergency, special)
  4. Click "Schedule" — all committee members are notified

6.3 Logging a Local Water Incident

  1. Open the Incidents tab in your committee's page
  2. Click "Add Incident"
  3. Enter: title, incident type (water_quality, pollution, borehole_failure, water_shortage, flood, sanitation), description, priority (low/medium/high/critical), and location
  4. Click "Save Incident" — it is sent to your District Officer for review

6.4 Managing Projects

  • Use the Projects tab to create and track community water and sanitation projects
  • Project types: Water, Sanitation, Environmental, Flood Response, Tree Planting, Borehole Repair
  • Track status (planned, in_progress, completed), budget, and timelines

6.5 Posting Announcements

  • Use the Announcements tab to post notices visible to all committee members
  • Include a title, body text, and optional priority flag

6.6 Other Access

Community Committee members can also access: Citizen Hub (/citizen-hub), GIS Map (/gis), Guardian Water Network (/gwn), Community Reports (/community), and the standard Reporting pages (/citizen-report, /multilingual-report, /track-reports).

🏛️ 7. District Officer Guide

As a District Officer, you oversee all water management activities within your district. You create committee member accounts, review citizen reports, assign tasks to technicians, issue alerts, and manage emergencies.

7.1 Creating a Community Committee Account

  1. Go to /committee-management and click the "Committee Accounts" tab
  2. Click "+ Create Account"
  3. Fill in: Full Name, Email Address, Phone, Temporary Password (min 6 characters)
  4. Select a District from the dropdown
  5. Choose the Committee Role: Chairperson, Secretary, Water Officer, Environmental Officer, Health Inspector, Emergency Coordinator, Community Mobilizer, District Supervisor, or Member
  6. Enter Jurisdiction Area (e.g. "Makindye Sub-County") and Organisation Name
  7. Click "Create Account"
  8. The system auto-generates a Committee ID (e.g. HSC-CC-KAM-2026-0001) — share this with the member for login

7.2 Reviewing Citizen Reports

  1. Go to /report-status to see all incoming citizen reports in your district
  2. Click a report to view full details: type, description, location, photos, and AI severity score
  3. Update the status: Under Review → Assigned → Resolved or Escalated
  4. For AI analysis, click "Analyze Report" to get Gemini AI's classification and risk score

7.3 Assigning Tasks to Technicians

  1. Navigate to /task-assignment
  2. Click "+ New Task" or use "Auto-Assign" to let the system distribute pending tasks intelligently
  3. For manual assignment: set task title, type, priority, due date, district, and select the technician
  4. Track task progress through Pending → In Progress → Completed

7.4 Issuing a Public Alert

  1. Go to /emergency
  2. Click "+ Create Alert"
  3. Set: Alert Type, Severity (Emergency / Critical / Warning / Info), District, Title, and Message
  4. Click "Create Alert" — citizens and staff in the district are notified immediately

7.5 Other District Officer Functions

  • Emergency Response (/emergency) — create and manage response tickets for field deployment
  • Governance (/governance) — review budget allocations, audit logs, and performance metrics for your district
  • Incident Command (/incident-command) — coordinate multi-agency emergency response
  • Analytics (/analytics) — view district water security scores, trends, and predictions
  • GIS Map (/gis) — spatial overview of all water points, drought zones, and flood risks in your district
  • Broadcast Notification — send district-wide announcements to all users in your district

🔧 8. Technician Guide

As a Technician, your primary workspace is the Technician Portal where you manage work orders, record sensor readings, and update infrastructure status.

8.1 Technician Portal (/technician-portal)

  • View tasks in three tabs: Pending Tasks, In Progress, Completed Today
  • Click a task to see full details: water point, issue type, priority, description, cost estimate
  • Click "Start Task" to move it to In Progress
  • Click "Mark Complete" when the work is done

8.2 IoT Sensors (/sensors)

  1. Navigate to /sensors to see the live sensor feed
  2. View real-time readings: current value, battery %, signal strength
  3. Check sensor status: Online (green), Low Battery (amber), Offline (red)
  4. Select a sensor to view its 24-hour reading chart
  5. To record a manual reading: select the sensor, click "Add Reading", and enter the measured value

8.3 Maintenance Requests (/maintenance)

  • View all maintenance requests assigned to you, filtered by status and priority
  • Create a new maintenance request: select the water point, describe the issue, set priority and estimated cost
  • Update request status as work progresses
  • Track spare parts inventory and maintenance fund balance

8.4 Water Infrastructure (/water-infrastructure)

  • Add new water points: enter name, type (borehole, protected spring, piped tap, etc.), status, GPS coordinates, beneficiary count, depth, pump type, and yield
  • Edit existing water point details after inspections
  • View the infrastructure map and filter by district, status, and type

8.5 AI Hub (/ai-hub)

Technicians have access to the AI Hub's Overview, Predictions, Anomaly Detection, and Smart Alerts tabs — useful for identifying which infrastructure is likely to fail and should be prioritised for maintenance.

🏥 9. Health Officer Guide

As a Health Officer, you monitor water-linked diseases, manage health incident records, and ensure safe water quality across your jurisdiction.

9.1 Logging a Disease Outbreak (/health)

  1. Go to /health and click "Log Health Incident"
  2. Select: District, Disease Type (cholera, typhoid, diarrhea, dysentery, hepatitis A, schistosomiasis)
  3. Enter: number of cases, deaths, hospitalisations, water source link (the suspected water point)
  4. Add investigation notes and click "Save Incident"
  5. Note: incidents with 10+ cases automatically trigger an outbreak alert to the district

9.2 Water Quality Testing (/water-quality)

  1. Go to /water-quality and click "Submit New Test"
  2. Select the Water Point being tested
  3. Enter test parameters: Turbidity (NTU), pH, TDS (mg/L), E. coli (CFU/100ml), Nitrates, Fluoride, Chlorine, Temperature
  4. The system automatically flags parameters that exceed WHO safe drinking-water standards
  5. Click "Submit Test" — a quality alert is raised if the source is unsafe

9.3 Incident Analysis (/incident-analysis)

  • Review pending citizen reports and run AI analysis to classify severity, risk score, and detect duplicates
  • Click "Analyze All Pending" to batch-process the queue with Gemini AI
  • View analysis results: AI category, risk score (0–100), confidence %, and recommended response

9.4 Emergency Response (/emergency)

  • Acknowledge and resolve emergency alerts related to water-borne disease outbreaks
  • Coordinate with district officers during multi-agency health emergencies

🌍 10. Climate Scientist Guide

10.1 Climate Monitoring (/climate)

  • Drought Index — view SPI (Standardised Precipitation Index) per district. Severity levels: Extreme, Severe, Moderate, Mild, Normal, Wet
  • Flood Risk Alerts — active flood risk zones with affected population estimates
  • 6-Month Forecast — AI-generated rainfall predictions by month
  • Climate Resilience Scores — radar chart showing each district's performance across: Water Access, Infrastructure, Climate Adaptation, Governance, Community Capacity
  • Weather Widget — live temperature, rainfall, and wind readings

10.2 Analytics (/analytics)

  • Overview tab — national water coverage %, people served, average quality score
  • Predictions tab — risk indicators for borehole failure, drought, flood, water demand surge, and contamination
  • Climate tab — 6-month trend charts for climate and maintenance data
  • Security tab — water security scores ranked by district, climate risk matrix

10.3 Incident Analysis (/incident-analysis)

Use AI-driven incident analysis to classify environmental events, detect patterns, and identify emerging risks. Run batch analysis on queued reports to accelerate response.

10.4 GIS Map (/gis)

Switch between the Drought Index layer and Flood Risk layer to visualise climate hazards spatially. Identify at-risk districts and water points for intervention planning.

🌿 11. NGO Officer Guide

11.1 Community & Environmental Work

  • Community Reports (/community) — review citizen-submitted reports and environmental observations; use Accept / Resolve actions
  • Guardian Water Network (/gwn) — submit pollution reports, vote on incident severity, view hotspot pollution maps
  • Citizen Reporting (/citizen-report, /multilingual-report) — submit detailed field reports from ground-level observations
  • Health Surveillance (/health) — view disease incident data and water safety trends
  • Water Quality (/water-quality) — access quality test records and safety statistics

11.2 GIS & Data Access

  • Full access to the GIS Map for spatial analysis of water points, drought, and flood zones
  • Access Citizen Hub to engage with community discussions and coordinate volunteer events
  • Track submitted reports via /track-reports and /report-status

👑 12. National Admin Guide

Warning: National Admin accounts have unrestricted access to all data and operations across all districts. Handle with care. All actions are recorded in the audit log.

12.1 User Management (/users)

  1. Go to /users to see the complete list of all platform users
  2. Use the Search bar to find a specific user by name
  3. Click "+ Create User" to add a new staff account (any role, any district)
  4. Click the edit icon on any user to update their name, phone, role, or district
  5. Click the key icon to reset a user's password
  6. Click the delete icon to remove an account (requires confirmation)
  7. Use the QR icon to generate a verifiable ID card for a staff member

12.2 Creating Committee Accounts

Unlike district officers (who are limited to their own district), National Admins can create committee accounts in any district. Follow the same process described in §7.1 — the district dropdown is fully open to all 141 districts.

12.3 Governance (/governance)

  • Budget tab — add and view budget records by project, funding source, and fiscal year; track allocation vs. expenditure
  • Performance tab — system-wide performance metrics across districts
  • Transparency tab — public accountability data and disclosure reports
  • Audit tab — complete audit trail of every CREATE, UPDATE, DELETE, APPROVE, RESOLVE, and DISPATCH action on the platform, with timestamp, actor, and affected record

12.4 System-Wide Operations

  • Full access to all 31 pages and all API endpoints
  • Incident Command (/incident-command) — create and escalate national-level incidents
  • AI Hub (/ai-hub) — all 9 AI intelligence tabs available
  • Broadcast Notifications — send system-wide alerts to all users or specific districts
