💧 HydroSense Introduction & System Overview
🌍

The Problem

Uganda faces a severe water and sanitation crisis. Over 60% of rural communities lack access to safe, reliable drinking water. Infrastructure breakdowns go unreported for weeks. Contamination events trigger disease outbreaks before health authorities are notified. Government response is fragmented — technicians, health officers, and district administrators work in silos with no shared platform.

The consequences are measurable: cholera outbreaks, waterborne illness, preventable deaths, and economic loss — all stemming from a lack of real-time information and coordination.

The Solution — HydroSense

HydroSense is a full-stack, enterprise-grade environmental monitoring platform that unifies IoT sensors, citizen reporters, field technicians, health officers, climate scientists, and national administrators into a single intelligent ecosystem — delivering real-time water quality intelligence, AI-powered incident response, and community-driven stewardship at scale.

📡

Real-Time Monitoring

Live IoT sensor feeds (pH, turbidity, TDS, flow) with automated alerts when thresholds are breached.

🤖

AI-Powered Intelligence

Gemini 2.5 Flash–driven chatbot, risk scoring, predictive analytics, and multilingual voice transcription.

🏘️

Community Integration

Citizens report via App, SMS, WhatsApp, Email, or voice in any of 18 Ugandan languages.

Key Implementation Highlights

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Every API endpoint is protected by a two-layer guard: JWT authentication middleware validates the token, then requireRole() enforces the minimum role required. The frontend mirrors this with a RoleRoute component that redirects unauthorised users before any data request is made.

Multi-Channel Citizen Reporting

Citizens can submit water quality or infrastructure incidents via the web app, SMS (Africa's Talking), WhatsApp, Email, or voice call. All submissions are normalised into the same citizen_reports table and trigger the same notification pipeline regardless of channel.

AI Intelligence (HYDRA)

The HYDRA AI service runs as a separate Python/FastAPI microservice. It connects to the PostgreSQL database directly, allowing Gemini 2.5 Flash to answer questions with real system data — current sensor readings, open incidents, and historical trends — rather than generic responses.

Real-Time Event Notification

Socket.IO broadcasts system events — new incidents, threshold breaches, and volunteer event alarms — to all connected clients. Role-based filters ensure each user receives only contextually relevant alerts, with popup + audio notification at 30 min, 5 min, and event start time.

💧 HydroSense Community Committee Management & Impact
🏘️

Community Committee Management Module

The Community Committee Management module closes the last-mile governance gap in rural water management. It gives district officers and community leaders a dedicated workspace to organise, track, and coordinate the work of water committees at the village, sub-county, and district level.

🏛️

Committee Registry

Create and manage water committees per district and sub-county, with chairperson assignment, meeting frequency, member count, and jurisdiction boundaries. Each committee has a full profile with status tracking (active / inactive / dissolved).

👥

Member Management

Enrol registered HydroSense users into committees with specific roles (Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, Member). Captures contact details, languages spoken, and join date. Members can be deactivated without removing their history.

📅

Meeting Management

Schedule meetings with date, time, and location. Record structured agendas, capture meeting minutes and resolutions, and mark individual attendance. Full meeting history is maintained per committee.

⚠️

Incident Assignments

Assign water quality or infrastructure incidents — including those submitted by citizens — directly to a committee for local investigation. Tracks status (new → investigating → resolved), priority level, evidence uploads, and escalation flag.

🔨

Community Projects

Track water-related community projects from planning to completion. Each project records type, budget, expenditure, progress percentage, start/end dates, and a lead officer. Enables transparent, accountable community development.

📢

Announcements

Post committee-level announcements with priority classification (Normal / Important / Urgent). Visible to all assigned members and district officers overseeing the committee area.

Impact & Conclusion

HydroSense demonstrates how a well-designed technology platform can bridge the coordination gaps that cost lives in rural water management. By unifying eight different stakeholder groups — from national administrators to ordinary citizens — onto a single real-time platform, it transforms fragmented, reactive governance into coordinated, data-driven stewardship.

The system is production-deployed, live on the internet, and built to real enterprise standards: role-based security, a microservices AI layer, multi-channel communication, and a fully normalised PostgreSQL database serving 35+ tables.
